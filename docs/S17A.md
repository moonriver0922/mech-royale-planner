# S17A guarded combat Boost and 45% recovery entry

## Policy and provenance

S17A descends directly from our scored S09 commit `77ca91a2a3499147bbe2532dfc88bb45f937f369`. It uses S09's combat growth selector, body-motion integrator, terrain clearance, shot geometry and held-command gun guard. The finite, stationary warning-ray comparison derives from our own S11 `internal/decision/burst.go` at commit `71d8b9460667df6eeb40b81a48bf9a5bc5919f58`. Public protocol, arena geometry and rule fields come from the official kit. The program depends only on Go's standard library.

Both S17 candidates perform the same comparison. S17A uses a strict HP-below-45% recovery entry and S09's 75% recovery exit. The four planned cells compare the two entry thresholds with and without guarded Boost.

## Decision

In either ordinary enemy-engagement branch, S09 first computes its action, including resource selection. A selected resource excludes defensive Boost even if it needs no Take and keeps precisely the original movement. The candidate requires a targeted player, a gun in the primary slot, an empty secondary slot, movement, grounded terrain feet within 0.1 m of the heightfield, current zone membership, and at least 0.30 seconds of observed Boost energy. It preserves S09's urgent-zone Boost, action fields, route ownership and laser state.

For each observed charging player, freeze its current cube-scaled muzzle and aim. Test a finite configured laser segment against the moving self cylinder and static geometry to first contact. From observed horizontal velocity, advance the original direction at both walking and Boost speeds for 0.30 s, with the configured ground acceleration and physics tick. The held action uses the same unit-disk normalization on both paths. Each path must remain terrain-walkable and inside the predicted circle on every endpoint. Boost must remove at least one ray/time intersection and add none.

Finally, S09's held-shot test replays the gun from the boosted origin on the first tick and every discharge due during the current held decision interval. It integrates the torso turn and extrapolates the observed target velocity. Only the Boost bit changes. This gun-only comparison runs after S09 computes reload and before laser memory is updated.

## Limits

The earlier S11 policy avoided one 315-damage beam and lost its aggregate local comparison. Avoiding damage can delay entry into recovery. S17 tests that interaction; either variant can still lower game score. The opponent may turn, release early or cancel, while the sampled ray model freezes its aim and omits release timing. Ground tracks omit collisions with moving actors and reject elevated supports. The held gun guard inherits S09's centerline, cooldown and linear-target assumptions. The 0.30 s tube is a comparison under a held direction; later decisions may change direction sooner. Checker timings measure protocol performance on a fixed recording.
