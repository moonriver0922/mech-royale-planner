# S01R — rear-threat acquisition and exposed return fire

## Design and provenance

The independent S00 ground controller at `19126d6516826c1de4e0e54c107c7bc4d6a23d81` supplies movement, navigation, resource selection, weapons and upgrades. S01R revises the integrated S01 experiment at `210df446db3e7019ce4f652197b616aa526b5f63`. Its inputs are the project's own code and observations plus the official protocol, configuration and map. The practice evaluator is an external measurement tool.

In approximate trace 40101, player 13 was absent from the tracked mechs during 19–27.8 seconds. That player caused 1,080 HP of the 1,215 HP damage after 20 seconds, with rear hits bearing a reported direction. S00 kept aiming at a visible opponent. S00 also suppressed return fire on routes where another opponent still had a clear shot.

## Decision rules

- Accumulate player damage by actual attacker ID, with 1.1-second exponential decay. Rear damage totaling 3.5% of max HP opens a 1.35-second acquisition window. Within active windows choose the highest decayed sum of total and rear damage, breaking ties by lower player ID. These thresholds are unchanged from the original S01.
- An unobserved attacker supplies only the reported world-frame yaw. A tracked attacker supplies its current position and uses the normal S00 shot geometry. A confirmed official `kill.target` ends acquisition for that player for the match, regardless of event order or later damage from a projectile already in flight. The protocol supplies no general identity for another player's unobserved elimination.
- Preserve urgent zone movement and recovery routes. If the tracked attacker takes over an engagement, compute the ordinary combat orbit around its reported position. A bearing alone leaves the nominal move, resource route, and any legal in-range pickup in place. A physical trigger is calculated only after choosing the final shot. The existing gun and lightning logic performs alignment, energy, reload and geometry checks.
- A route to improved cover permits legal return fire while exposed. A stationary regeneration hold requires zero current observed exposure and no active bearing-only attacker. A zone exit still allows valid return fire.
- Approximation limits include motion of an unseen attacker during the acquisition window, uncertainty about cover against distant unobserved players, and terrain/collision limits of the original ground controller.

## Excluded movement experiment

Frozen S01 evaluated ten commands over 0.5 seconds with acceleration, nearby projectiles and endpoint exposure. On 16 examined approximate practice starts, S00 averaged 4.0000 placement points and full S01 averaged 3.5625. Disabling only the movement rollout scored 4.9375, whereas disabling only threat priority scored 4.5000. The no-rollout variant changed three outcomes against S00, gaining 7 and 13 points and losing 5. An independent review also found that modeled exposure to an idle or distant actor could outweigh route progress. S01R therefore removes that evaluation and its dedicated tests. The original implementation, its tests and its design remain available at the frozen S01 commit.

## Verification and execution

Tests exercise hidden and acquired attackers, deterministic ties, expiry and confirmed kills, legal firing during cover transitions and urgent zone exit, retained pickup and detour, and changed orbit under a switch of combat target. The official native checker measures protocol conformance and local timing. Before examining its results, the fresh paired validation range was fixed at 41001–41032. The older 16 worlds are development evidence. Replaying fixed observations describes commands but cannot establish match outcomes. Official submission and receipt handling remain a separate root-owned action.
