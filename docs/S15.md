# S15 · Preserve a loaded gun during a brief loss of sight

S15 branches directly from S09 at `77ca91a2a3499147bbe2532dfc88bb45f937f369`. The frozen design covers one decision. When S09 would top off a partially filled gun, S15 defers that request if the current observation reports a player (`kind == "mech"`, `hp > 0`) and the remaining rounds times the effective firing interval reach the effective full reload duration. Each effective stat comes from the gun's reported stats with its hello configuration as fallback. This uses the per-shot interval and the full reload duration, independently of either remaining countdown. The decision introduces no timer or stored actor history.

With the standard 12-round gun, a blocked player can defer a top-off at 10 or 11 rounds. At 9 rounds the parent can start the reload. Ten rounds cover ten nominal firing slots at 0.24 s each. A ready gun could fire its tenth round after 2.16 s. This budget estimates the value of keeping rounds ready and does not predict future firing opportunities. Sustained obstruction can cost a chance to refill. When the protocol omits enemy HP, the inherited wire model decodes it as zero and the guard does not apply.

On three previously selected S09 traces, the rule selected six of 17 partial reloads, all in 45017. At 39.9 s S09 requested a reload at 10/12 while two players remained tracked. At 40.1 s clearing just the recorded reload lock in a one-frame probe enabled the gun's trigger. The probe does not measure damage or placement.
