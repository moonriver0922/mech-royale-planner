## Frozen implementation

The original combat selection marks whether the selected visible actor is a player. Both ordinary engagement branches run the burst check only after final fire/reload selection and S02 laser bookkeeping. The check stores no state. A legal laser start or held trigger therefore slows step one, including at observed charge zero. A queued release slows the model for max(0, min_charge − observed_charge) + one physics step. Short and cooldown suppress this penalty.

At 20 Hz the six sample times are 0.05 through 0.30 s, every sample weighted equally. Normalize the original Move only if its norm exceeds one. For each speed mode, accelerate the observed horizontal velocity toward speed × movement multiplier × Move, limiting the Euclidean velocity change to ground_accel / tick_hz per step. Integrate using the updated velocity. The locally constant inputs are the original Move, the two alternative speed modes and all warning rays. The burst horizon requires 0.30 × boost_drain energy plus the energy of an issued lightning pulse. No regeneration is credited.

The shooter uses the observed feet, actual yaw/pitch, cube-scaled muzzle height, and forward offset of cube-scaled radius + 0.5 m. Check clear terrain and prisms to the muzzle, finite cylinder intersection within the hello laser range, then line of sight to first body contact. Our body uses the observed effective radius and height. Walk and Boost must both remain in the time-interpolated circle and pass grounded map checks at every sample. The existing swept body check verifies walkability. The center-foot check verifies the terrain or supporting roof top, with 0.03 m for rounded input coordinates. It rejects roof edge and hole crossings, uncertain near-edge support, and drops over step_up.

A ray intersecting Boost at any sampled step where the same ray misses Walk vetoes the action. At least one ray must hit Walk and miss Boost at some sample. These two requirements imply a strict reduction in total hit samples, and include every visible charging player. They require neither a release forecast nor a duration estimate for the enemy's charge. The official 20 Hz match yields exactly six physics steps. Unrepresentable horizons or more than 32 steps fall back to walking. The next decision evaluates its new observation independently.
