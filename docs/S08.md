## Frozen decision rule

Compute S02's ordinary target first, in its original observation order. A pressure override is eligible only for a currently reported player with a positive HP reading, a valid S02 solution, and at least one nominal impact sequence that can finish the target from the current range and geometry. The original target, obscured-charge fallback, and ordinary scanning behavior apply when no override qualifies.

For player i, let W be our observed regen_delay in seconds with the hello configuration fallback. Each positive damaged event with an external player ID contributes its actual amount multiplied by (1 − age/W) while 0 ≤ age < W. Zone, NPC, anonymous, self-attributed and invalid-ID damage contribute zero. Same-player events in one observation are combined. A confirmed kill clears that player's history for the match, including later posthumous projectiles. The implementation bounds storage to 128 distinct observed event times per player, compared with 50 decision intervals in the usual five-second window.

The score is that weighted incoming damage divided by max(one decision interval, estimated seconds to finish). A positive score wins over targets with no attributable pressure. Equal scores prefer the current enemy target, followed by S02's existing ordering. There is no damage-bearing aim override.

The estimate merges the next impacts from the two equipped supported weapons against current target HP. Each nominal hit uses observed weapon damage (configuration fallback) times 1 + damage_per_cube × min(own cubes, stat_cap). The first impact includes the larger of the required yaw/pitch slew and weapon readiness. Gun readiness includes observed cooldown, any remaining reload, or a whole reload when empty. Gun impact also includes distance to the led point divided by projectile speed. Later gun shots follow cooldown with a max(reload duration, cooldown) interval across each magazine boundary. A laser first finishes its remaining charge after cooldown/short clears, then delivers full-charge impacts at cooldown + full charge; a pending release is excluded. Lightning uses yaw, current center range and line of sight, cooldown, and at most the shots affordable from current energy. The first impact that brings nominal cumulative damage to the observed HP gives the estimate. Work stops at 128 impacts per player. Unavailable or insufficient streams yield no override.

Before switching, preview the original fire arbitration against S02's target. Preserve its nominally lethal volley, counting enabled gun shots, direct lightning not intercepted by a nearer visible actor/crate, and a live laser actually released with sufficient charge and a clear ray. An active charge stays on its observed current actor when a proposed transfer fails either the current or next-physics-tick hitscan test. These two tests include static clearance and the number of intervening observed actors/crates allowed by the equipped beam's pierce stat. Missing observations never create an invented target position.

The estimate assumes present geometry, steady weapon timing and successful nominal hits. It omits opponent armor, orientation bonuses, cook-off, gun spread, future movement or energy replenishment, and future shared-aim contention. Forecast lightning assumes a direct hit while the immediate finish guard checks competing recipients. The estimate can therefore overstate or understate actual removal speed. A small residual attributable threat can still win over a fresh but unattributed opponent. Existing movement, resource, recovery and trigger execution remain S02's. Protocol conformance and synthetic fixtures cannot establish a placement improvement.
