## Implemented model

The controller applies the defense after both triggers, aim, loot and reload are finalized. The observed horizontal projectile segment first has to approach a disc containing every reachable mech center and its current body radius. For each physics tick the forecast moves the actual velocity toward the commanded velocity by at most ground acceleration times tick duration. The walkable map segment supplies its terminal support height, including prism tops. For elevated footing, the forecast requires the center to remain on a continuous supporting footprint through each map-cell segment. Crossing or grazing a roof or hole boundary can involve an edge slide, so the defense omits those transitions. Existing global route passability retains its original behavior. A blocked original path or a drop larger than step height leaves the original command in place because wall slides and airborne transitions are outside the model.

The simulator reuses S02's finite cylinder entry calculation in the moving body's frame. It expands the radius and vertical bounds by the configured gun or NPC projectile radius. This bounds the rounded cap corners conservatively. Static occlusion checks the projectile centerline from its observed position to first body contact, so obstacles behind contact do not prevent a hit. Each visible round contributes its nominal configured direct damage once. Unknown travel before the observation, enemy upgrades, dynamic interceptors, rear-armor changes, subsequent control inputs, and unobserved shots are not predicted.

An issued laser hold slows a newly starting charge immediately. An existing hold uses the configured forced-release deadline, and a queued release uses the remaining minimum charge. Boost alternatives reserve one shot's energy for each already-triggered lightning slot and the full horizon of boost drain, with no regeneration credit. In-zone alternatives must remain in the interpolated circle at every physics step. Outside alternatives must match or improve the original signed zone margin at each step and keep any urgent original boost.

The eight walking directions use a fixed compass ordering beginning east and continuing counterclockwise. The other three options boost along the original command or perpendicular to the earliest predicted incoming path. Strictly lower nominal damage is required to override the original, then eligible alternatives are ordered by energy, signed displacement along the original command, and generation order. These estimates are a mechanism check. A change in placement requires fresh games.
