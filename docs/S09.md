## Mechanism
S02's immediately ready combat branch prevents resource movement even when the same enemy could still be fired on while moving toward a nearby useful item. Test a targeted alternative: while actively engaging a player, preserve its target, Aim and all firing decisions, but permit a short, directly walkable movement toward a loose cube below the current stat cap or a supported secondary weapon when the slot is empty.

Candidate item must lie within three seconds of current achievable walking travel, remain inside the projected zone on arrival, and be reachable without a route search or a roof/ledge traversal beyond existing ground capability. Use current position pickup rules, never early movement-then-pickup requests. Do not pursue crates or mortar in this change. When no eligible item exists, retain S02's combat action exactly.

Preserve the planned shot opportunity: reject a resource direction if the next physics-step origin/body motion would block an issued gun or laser shot, accounting for observed velocity and acceleration. Validate the entire first held decision interval, including a gun cooldown or a queued laser minimum that clears after the resource is reached. Do not create a new charge/aim executor. Do not replace urgent-zone or covered-recovery movement. Validate terrain clearance, projected zone membership and visible lightning range through every tick of the initial held move, including ticks after the item is reached and decisions with no shot due. Do not move into a visible lightning carrier's weapon range when the original move would remain outside it. Preserve combat target/path ownership; do not replace enemy goal with an item goal merely to get a movement vector. Legal Take can be added for the selected item when already in range and an eligible empty slot exists.

Prefer by actual travel time to collection, stable ID tie; freeze this one bounded policy before new-game results. This is a productive-combat experiment, not a promise that short detours are free. Report motion/shot model limits explicitly.
