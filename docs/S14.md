# S14 independent crossfire experiment

S14 changes movement in a losing gun-only player engagement. Its exact parent is official S09 commit `77ca91a2a3499147bbe2532dfc88bb45f937f369`, tree `f88024cd5a1a019c199836affa507b2513410a45`. Source inputs were this project's S09 implementation, the supplied official mechfight-kit protocol and public map/config, and the project's written crossfire research and historical observations. Only observations from the S11 recording are used in an optional test. The implementation uses Go's standard library and S09's own map, steering, combat and held-shot helpers.

## Entry

1. Complete S09's ordinary decision, including target, route, aim, triggers, item/upgrade commands and reload. A new destination can be selected only on a player engagement branch with a positively observed target HP, grounded in-zone self at or above 45% HP, a gun in slot 0, an empty slot 1, no recovery latch and no Take.
2. Aggregate positive observed player damage in `(now - base gun reload, now]` by attacker ID. The official protocol assigns players IDs 0–15. Null, self and out-of-range IDs are excluded. Only currently visible actors of kind `mech` with a clear static ray from their scaled muzzle height to the center of our body can contribute. Require two distinct sources. The damage ring holds at most one interval of decision bins (24 × 16 amounts under the supplied configuration). Observation timestamps expire old damage after skipped ticks.
3. For a confirmed source carrying and charging a laser, test its observed aim, one physics tick of legal slew, and a full decision of legal slew toward our center. Apply public charging turn/pitch rates and pitch limits. Test a finite ray from the scaled barrel offset to first intersection with our observed body cylinder, including clearance along the muzzle segment. Credit at most one burst, the largest full base charge × capped cube damage scale × observed self armor reduction. Lower source ID breaks an equal-damage tie. Enemy body dimensions scale with all cubes.
4. Estimate our earliest finish by treating every future gun round as an immediate perfect rear hit after its flight. Use effective gun stats, capped cube damage scaling, current ammo/cooldown/remaining reload, full magazines and `max(cooldown, reload)` at magazine transitions. The optimistic estimate counts transitions in constant time. Final flight distance is `max(0, shot.distance − target.radius − self.radius − 0.5)`. Protect every target within one such round. The incoming forecast uses `max(0, self.HP − burst) / (confirmed damage / window)`. Require forecast lethality within twice the *base* regeneration delay (10 s) and a finish more than one decision later.
5. Examine 12 m then 24 m ground destinations at eight stable angles around our torso. Require a directly body-walkable segment, an in-zone destination at estimated arrival, at least one newly blocked confirmed ray and no opened ray from a player whose ray started blocked. When only the estimated beam made the forecast lose, the selected destination must occlude that source. Rank by occluding the credited beam, then number of confirmed rays cut, shortest distance and angle order. Test any issued gun shot through the entire held command using S09’s existing movement/shot projection.

## Continuation and limits

Steer directly toward the retained destination, decreasing command magnitude near arrival. The deadline is the original estimated travel time plus two decision periods. Recover, urgent zone, ordinary resource or crate actions, legal Take, losing a gun-only grounded/in-zone state, an NPC engagement, a one-round player finish, arrival within 1 m, expiry, a blocked walk, an unsafe endpoint on arrival, a lost required laser occlusion or a threatened issued shot ends the intervention. A temporary disappearance of the credited source is expected and can retain the walk, including briefly during ordinary scouting. Recheck a required laser source whenever visible. A fresh confirmed damage tick must follow the last successfully used evidence tick before another start. S09 still computes its complete normal action and maintains its own navigation and 45%/75% recovery logic on every decision.

A visible charge does not expose its age or guarantee a future release or hit. Endpoint occlusion is a static center-ray estimate. It gives no corridor safety guarantee and enemies may move, turn, upgrade or redirect their fire. Other mechs and collision impulses are outside the traversal forecast. The optimistic finish omits existing in-flight bullets and third-party damage. Leaving S09's original movement can cost nearby cubes. Historical witnesses were found on one seed and cannot establish score improvement.

## Regression inputs

The standard Go suite includes synthetic map, budget, race, priority, shot-preservation, bounded-history and continuation tests. Set `S14_S09_TRACE` and `S14_S11_TRACE` to the respective archived seed 45002 observation JSONL paths to also run the original S09 21.7 s movement witness and the S11 22.9 s actual-hit witness. Set `S14_ARENA` to the official arena directory if the path in the recorded hello is unavailable. These fixed observations do not simulate consequences of divergent actions.
